# Privacy Policy for FlippyTrail

*Last updated: 3 October 2026*

FlippyTrail is a free, single-player arcade game published by Craig Hillsdon.

## Data the app itself collects

None. FlippyTrail has no accounts, no login, and no way to enter personal
information. Your score and best score are stored only on your own device
and are never sent anywhere.

## Data collected via advertising

FlippyTrail shows ads through Google AdMob, which is how it earns revenue.
To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

- Your device's advertising ID
- General device information (device type, operating system version)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, clicked, or dismissed)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by FlippyTrail directly,
and is used to select and measure the ads you see. If you are in the UK or
EEA, you will be asked to give or decline consent to this the first time you
open the app, in line with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device's
system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID,
or Opt out of Ads Personalization, depending on your Android version).

For more detail on how Google uses this data, see
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Data retention

FlippyTrail does not operate any servers and retains no data of its own.
Any data handled by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to
Google's own policies, linked above.

## Children

FlippyTrail is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data
from children.

## Contact

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's use of advertising changes. The
"Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
